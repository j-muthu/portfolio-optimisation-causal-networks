# Thesis project instructions

## Workflow
- Do NOT rebuild the report with latexmk (or pdflatex). The user compiles it
  themselves; only edit the raw .tex files.

## Review mindset
- When reviewing or editing the report, adopt the mindset of the editor of a
  good CS journal / a strong CS researcher. The aim is a distinction-level
  thesis (the user already holds a distinction in exams and wants to continue
  that standard). Hold claims to referee standards: check that every claim is
  earned by the evidence, that stated mechanisms are actually tested, and that
  framing matches what the chapters establish.

## Writing style
- No em-dashes except where clearly necessary.
- UK English, first person, plain declarative sentences.
