# Pre-committed interpretations: HERC second family member

Committed BEFORE any HERC configuration was run. Chapter 2 argues the
interface deficit is family-wide: ANY hierarchical allocator that
clusters on a symmetric matrix discards the same directional information,
whatever its tree-reading rule. Every experiment so far tests one family
member (HRP). This arm tests a second.

## Construction

Full-recursion HERC (Raffinot 2018 simplified: no cluster-count cut,
inverse-cluster-variance splits, the same cluster-risk measure as HRP's
bisection), so the ONLY change from the HRP family is how the dendrogram
is read: HRP reads it through the quasi-diagonal leaf ordering, HERC
walks its actual topology top-down. Three cells, DYNOTEARS, four windows:

- HERCC: correlation-distance HERC (graph-blind control).
- HERC0: D0's embedding distance, sample covariance (skeleton member;
  unit-verified orientation-blind under edge transposition).
- HERC1: D0's distance, Sigma_SEM (orientation member; unit-verified
  orientation-sensitive).

Quantities read per window: skeleton = HERC0 - HERCC, orientation =
HERC1 - HERC0, mirroring D0 - CORR and D1 - D0.

## Pre-committed interpretations

1. **Signs of both channels broadly match the HRP family's** (skeleton
   contribution concentrated near the one-year window, orientation
   increment non-negative at most windows): the decomposition
   generalises beyond HRP's bisection; the family-wide framing of
   Chapter 2 is supported by a second member and will be stated as
   tested, not argued.
2. **HERC channels are flat or negative where HRP's were positive**: the
   decomposition is a property of HRP's bisection interface, not of
   hierarchical allocation per se; Chapter 2's family-wide claim will be
   weakened to "tested on two members, holds on one", and the
   limitations section will say so.
3. **Mixed**: reported as bounding the generalisation, with the cells
   shown; no cell will be dropped.

These cells join the deflation universe (they are evaluated trials) and
NEITHER pre-existing SPA family (both families were fixed long before
this arm existed); no new family-wise test will be constructed around
them after results are seen. Point estimates and pairwise bootstrap
contrasts only.
