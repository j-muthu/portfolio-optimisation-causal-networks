\begin{abstract}
\noindent Hierarchical portfolio allocators cluster assets on a symmetric
matrix --- usually the return correlation matrix, blind to which asset
moves which. Temporal causal discovery
yields a richer input: a directed acyclic graph, carrying a \emph{skeleton}
(which pairs are linked) and \emph{edge orientations} (which way influence
flows); symmetric clustering keeps only the first. The thesis asks one
question: \emph{when the correlation matrix in hierarchical portfolio
allocation is replaced by a discovered causal graph, how much of the
performance gain is attributable to the graph's skeleton versus its edge
orientations?}

A fixed-graph ablation answers it. At each of 215 monthly rebalances over
2007--2024 on the ${\sim}100$ largest S\&P~500 constituents, one DYNOTEARS
graph (VARLiNGAM and ridge-Granger as comparators) feeds a graph-blind
correlation-distance HRP control, a skeleton-only allocator, and three
orientation-aware allocators built on the DAG's total-effect matrix --- all
deterministic and bit-reproducible. Performance means out-of-sample,
net-of-cost annualised Sharpe at four estimation horizons (nine months to
two years), adjudicated by a
PSR/DSR/Reality-Check/SPA/MCS battery over all \rsNtrials\ configurations
evaluated.

The answer: the graph's total gain over the control is stable across
horizons but never individually significant --- the best orientation-aware
allocator earns $+0.018$ to $+0.032$ net Sharpe over the control at every
window ($p=0.43$--$0.12$), and 36 of the 40 graph-versus-control cells
(all allocator arms, both discovery methods, four windows) are positive
--- but its \emph{composition} shifts. In point
estimates across the four windows, the skeleton's rung is largest near the
one-year convention ($-0.003$, $+0.022$, $+0.018$, $+0.009$), while the
orientation rung is positive at \emph{every} horizon and largest at the
extremes ($+0.021$, $+0.009$, $+0.012$, $+0.023$), where sample moments are
weakest. Orientation thus acts as a \emph{regulariser}, substituting causal
structure for sample-moment quality; the orientation-aware allocators
attain the study's two highest deflated Sharpes ($\rsDtwosDSRWthree$,
$\rsDoneDSRWthree$). The support for this composition is directional rather
than inferential: one of twelve orientation contrasts reaches $p<0.05$
uncorrected --- close to what multiplicity alone predicts --- no rung survives
family-wise correction (SPA: skeleton
$p=\rsSpaSkelWfive$--$\rsSpaSkelWone$, orientation
$p=\rsSpaDirWfive$--$\rsSpaDirWtwo$), and the effect is absent under
VARLiNGAM.

Two supporting results sharpen the verdict: the incumbent HSP driver route
has an unlearnable monthly reward (signal-to-noise
${\approx}\,\rsRewardSNR$) and is seed-unstable (Sharpe
$\rsSeedMin$--$\rsSeedMax$ over \rsSeedN\ seeds --- twice its claimed
edge); the graph route is deterministic and exceeds its best seed. The
contribution is a controlled, reproducible decomposition of where a causal
graph's value lies: in the skeleton only near the one-year convention, in
the orientations at every horizon, and nowhere past a family-wise bar.
\end{abstract}

\chapter*{Declarations}

\section*{Statement of originality}
This report and the work it describes are my own. The research was conducted
under the supervision of Dr.\ Ce Guo and Prof.\ Wayne Luk. All external
sources --- published work, datasets and third-party code --- are cited in
the text; vendored third-party libraries are identified as such in the
repository. All numerical results are reproducible from the committed code
and cached data (Appendix~\ref{app:tables}).

\section*{Ethical considerations}
No Imperial ethics approval was required: the project uses only publicly
available market and macroeconomic data and involves no human participants or
personal data. The work is a retrospective backtest study; it makes no
investment recommendations, and Chapter~\ref{chap:evaluation} quantifies
precisely the uncertainty with which its results should be read.

\section*{Use of generative AI}
Generative AI (Anthropic's Claude) was used in two capacities, both reviewed
by me as author. \emph{As a coding assistant} during implementation: all
AI-assisted code was human-reviewed and is covered by the project's test
suite and by the end-to-end verification gates of
Section~\ref{sec:ablation-design} --- including a byte-perfect replication
gate against an independently produced historical result --- so no
correctness claim rests on unverified generated code. \emph{As a drafting and
proofreading aid} for the prose of this report: AI-drafted text was checked
and edited line by line, and, because unverified numerical claims are a known
failure mode of generated text, every statistic quoted in this report is
either a macro generated directly from the committed results
(\texttt{\_generated/robust\_stats.tex}) or traceable to a committed results
file, as documented in Appendix~\ref{app:tables}. The research question,
design decisions, mathematics, experiments, analysis and conclusions are my
own.
