\section{Robust-stats battery}
\label{app:robust}

Table~\ref{tab:robust} gives the per-variant Probabilistic and Deflated Sharpe
ratios behind Section~\ref{sec:believe-rigour} for the one-year headline universe;
the full \rsNtrials-configuration table (every variant $\times$ method $\times$
window $\times$ $K$ $\times$ feedback cell) is in \texttt{results/robust\_stats.csv},
regenerated by \texttt{python -m scripts.robust\_stats}. PSR is the probability
the true Sharpe exceeds zero given the sample's skewness, kurtosis and length;
DSR deflates that benchmark to the best Sharpe expected from \rsNtrials\ trials
under the null. The data-snooping tests (best causal variant vs V0, one-year)
give White's Reality Check $p=\rsSpaRC$ and Hansen's SPA $p=\rsSpaConsistent$; the
$90\%$ Model Confidence Set contains \rsMcsSize\ variants (\rsMcsMembers).

\section{Hyperparameters}

For reproducibility, the operative settings were: DYNOTEARS lag $p=1$, intra/
inter-slice penalties $\lambda_W=\lambda_A=0.05$, edge threshold $0.01$; the
sensitivity network searched depth $\in\{1,2\}$ and width $\in\{16,32,64\}$ by
held-out RMSE; $K=17$ from Kneedle calibration on the 2006 burn-in; closed loop
$\alpha=0.6$, $\gamma=0.3$ (default), swept over
$\alpha\in\{0.4,0.6,0.8\}\times\gamma\in\{0.1,0.3,0.5\}$; transaction cost
5\,bps one-way; lookback windows 252 and 504 trading days; holding and rebalance
step 21 trading days; 215 rebalances over 2007-01 to 2024-11; significance by
Politis--Romano stationary block bootstrap with 2000 resamples.

\section{Reproducibility}

All results derive from the committed code and cached data, and are
bit-reproducible following the determinism fix of Section~\ref{sec:cache}. The
headline matrix is regenerated by the full-sample backtest launcher across the
four variants and two windows; the $K$-sweep and $\alpha,\gamma$ grid reuse the
content-keyed discovery cache; the figures are regenerated from the persisted
result bundles and summary CSVs by the plotting scripts. No result depends on a
live network fetch: the universe, prices and driver series are all cached.
