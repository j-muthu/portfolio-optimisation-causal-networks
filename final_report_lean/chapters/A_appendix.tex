\section{Hyperparameters}

For reproducibility, the operative settings. \emph{Discovery:} DYNOTEARS lag
$p=1$, $\lambda_W=\lambda_A=0.05$, edge threshold $0.01$; VARLiNGAM with
lingam pruning disabled (adaptive lasso fails at $d>n$); GRANGER ridge
$\lambda=10^{-2}$ scaled by window length, density-matched per window to the
paired DYNOTEARS graph. \emph{Allocators:} single-linkage where clustering is
used; $\Sigma_{\mathrm{struct}}$ with diagonal shock variances from
fit-window residuals, nearest-PSD projection and ridge load
$10^{-6}\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma)/N$; ERC by cyclical coordinate descent,
tolerance $10^{-12}$, parity asserted to $10^{-8}$; topological tie-break by
total downstream influence then name. \emph{Driver route:} $K=17$ (Kneedle,
2006 burn-in); sensitivity networks searched depth $\{1,2\}\times$ width
$\{16,32,64\}$ by held-out RMSE; feedback grid
$\alpha\in\{0.4,0.6,0.8\}\times\gamma\in\{0.1,0.3,0.5\}$.
\emph{Protocol:} 5\,bps one-way (swept $\{0,5,10,20\}$); windows 252/504;
holding and step 21 trading days; 215 rebalances 2007-01--2024-11;
Politis--Romano bootstrap, 2000 resamples, mean block 21.

\section{Reproducibility}

All results derive from committed code and cached data and are
bit-reproducible: after the data-determinism fix of Section~\ref{sec:cache},
two independent end-to-end runs produce byte-identical graphs, weights and
NAVs, verified by the replication gate (D0 vs the prior phase's committed
bundle: $\Delta$Sharpe $= 0$, max weight difference $= 0.0$ over all 215
rebalances). The ablation ran on 836 cache-verified graph fits with a 100\%
hit rate; each backtest regenerates in ${\approx}40$ seconds from the cache.
Every statistic quoted in the text is either a generated macro
(\texttt{\_generated/robust\_stats.tex}, rebuilt by
\texttt{scripts/robust\_stats.py -{}-phase-ii}) or traceable to a committed
CSV (\texttt{results/phase\_ii\_matrix.csv},
\texttt{phase\_ii\_contrasts.csv}, \texttt{robust\_stats\_phase\_ii.csv},
\texttt{seed\_audit.csv}, \texttt{regime\_analysis/}); figures are
regenerated by \texttt{scripts/plot\_phase\_ii\_figures.py}. No result
depends on a live network fetch.
